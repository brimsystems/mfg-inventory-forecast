Manufacturing Data Platform: Inventory Forecasting & ERP Data Quality

Deliverables for a precision machining shop, from a dirty item master to forecast-driven reorder points.

Seven deliverables trace the work end to end: a diagnosis of where the item master is dirty and what it costs, an entity-resolution and cleaning pass, a demand model that forecasts each item over its lead time, that forecast delivered as reorder suggestions inside the purchasing screen, an inventory policy that turns accuracy into working capital, and the MLOps monitoring that keeps it honest. Full detail is in the README.
