Section 1

Status & Retraining Decision

The demand forecasting model has been monitored monthly across its live window, January to June 2026. Forecast error and bias are the primary model triggers (i.e., when these fail in two consecutive months, the model must be retrained), while target, prediction and feature drift are leading indicators. Business KPIs including fill rates, stockout events and jobs held are also tracked in this report.

A forecast can only be scored once its lead-time window has closed, so each month is judged on accuracy once at least 80% of its forecasts have matured. By June 30th that covers January to April; May and June are shown but not yet judged on accuracy.

The flag reads RETRAIN RECOMMENDED, meaning the model should be fully retrained on data through June. This is because the model's forecast error on intermittent items drifted outside tolerance in two consecutive months (March and April). Separately, the safety buffers should be recalibrated: achieved fill rates ran more than 1 point below their targets for the five months between January and May.

The layers below track the model every month. Performance is measured against the held-out 2025 year, drift against the rows the model was trained on, and outcomes against the shop's 2025 results.

Section 2.1

Performance

Forecast error each month for the bias-corrected forecasts the reorder points use, against the 45.7% reference from the held-out 2025 year. Across the matured months, overall error stays close to the reference (45.1% to 47.1%) and overall bias stays within 5%, so the model as a whole has not degraded. The later months' figures come from the few forecasts already matured, mostly items with short lead times, so they are not comparable yet.

Section 2.2

Target Drift

Distance between each month's actual lead-time usage and the training rows (Jensen-Shannon, flagged at 0.10). A shift would mean demand itself has moved away from what the model learned. Across the matured months target drift stays well under the threshold (0.031 to 0.045): the shop's usage looks like the usage the model was trained on. The higher readings for May and June reflect the short-lead-time items that make up their few matured forecasts, not a shift in demand.

Section 2.6

Business Outcomes

The outcomes the reorder policy is accountable for, from the live replay. Fill rate is measured against each criticality group's target. At least one group ran more than 1 point below its target in 5 of 6 months, by as much as 2.6 points, narrowing to 0.6 points by Jun 2026. Overall forecast bias is small, so the shortfall points to the safety buffers: calibrated on 2025 errors, they are not quite reaching the targets, and should be recalibrated on the January to June errors. The early months also carry the handover from the manual stock levels.
